# Customer voice: how outbound messages sound

Used by the planner when it drafts message text, and by the critic when it reviews it.

- Reply in the customer's language (`en` or `ar`). Arabic is clear Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally in the Gulf.
- Two or three short sentences: what happened, what we did, what they need to do (usually nothing).
- Name the exact amount and currency that the plan refunds. Never promise anything the plan doesn't do.
- One apology, no blame, no internal words (no "ticket", "plan", "agent", "simulation").
- No links unless the plan includes one. Never ask for card details or passwords.
- Sign off as the brand team, not as a person.
